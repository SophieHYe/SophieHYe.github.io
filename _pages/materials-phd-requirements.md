The UCL PhD is a **four-year** programme. Below is what I expect from students in my
group before they graduate.

## Publication requirement

A student may graduate with **any one** of the following combinations:

1. **3** SE CCF-A papers
2. **2** SE CCF-A papers **+ 2** AI top-tier papers
3. **1** SE CCF-A paper **+ 3** AI top-tier papers
4. **4** AI top-tier papers

## Which venues count

- **SE CCF-A** (research track only, or the corresponding journal): ICSE, ASE, FSE, ISSTA, TSE, TOSEM — see the
  [CCF SE venue ranking](https://www.ccf.org.cn/Academic_Evaluation/TCSE_SS_PDL/).
- **AI top-tier**: ICML, ICLR, NeurIPS, ACL Main, CVPR, etc. — see the
  [CCF AI venue ranking](https://www.ccf.org.cn/Academic_Evaluation/AI/).

Track the submission deadlines for all of these at [CCF Deadlines](https://ccfddl.com/).

## Authorship rule

A paper only counts towards graduation if **both** hold:

- the student is the **first author, in first position**, and
- I am the **last author and the corresponding author**.
