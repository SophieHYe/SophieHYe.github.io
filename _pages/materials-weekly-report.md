This is the template every student uses to report to me. We expect **one slide deck
per week**, with the four sections below.

## 1. What has been done last week

## 2. Five papers I have read last week

One entry per paper, each covering:

- **Title** — with a link.
- **What is the paper about?** — one sentence.
- **Novelty of this paper** — one sentence.
- **Strengths and weaknesses** — one bullet point each.
- **Similarity to our work** — one sentence.
- **Difference from our work** — one sentence.

## 3. How does last week's work affect or change the position of our current work?

Go through all four:

1. **Problem / challenge / gap**
2. **Novelty**
3. **Contribution**
4. **Solution**
