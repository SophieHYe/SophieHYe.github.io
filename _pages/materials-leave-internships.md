## Leave

- **28 days of annual leave per year**, no work expected on those days.
- On top of that, **two extra weeks of remote-work flexibility** — you keep working,
  just not from the office.

One principle: **no sudden, short-notice leave right before a deadline.** Absent
humanitarian or compassionate reasons, leave announced at the last minute in the
run-up to a submission will not be approved.
Plan it around the calendar instead — see the deadlines on the
[graduation requirements](/materials/phd-requirements/) page.

## Internships

I **encourage** you to do internships, as many as you like — as long as you keep your
own progress on track and still meet the
[graduation requirements](/materials/phd-requirements/).

Two things are not optional:

- the **period and dates** of the internship need your supervisors' approval, and
- you need the university's **off-campus approval** before you go.
